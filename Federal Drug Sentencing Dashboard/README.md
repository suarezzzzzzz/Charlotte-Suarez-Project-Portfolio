# Federal Drug Sentencing Dashboard

*Partner: Medhavi Jambhekar*

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Race, Medicine, and Incarceration course during my senior year at The University of Texas at Austin. The project investigated historical disparities in federal crack and powder cocaine sentencing and translated the findings into an interactive dashboard designed to facilitate exploration and provide historical context.

The project required substantial data preparation and subject-matter research. The original dataset contained nearly 600 variables, requiring me to use SPSS to parse, clean, and identify the variables relevant to the research question. I also conducted historical and policy research and reviewed sentencing-related variables to understand their meaning and determine which measures were appropriate for analysis.

## Research Objective

**Investigate historical disparities in federal crack and powder cocaine sentencing.**

After identifying and preparing the relevant data in SPSS, I used R to analyze sentencing trends and develop an interactive Shiny dashboard. The dashboard allows users to explore patterns in sentencing outcomes and demographic characteristics while providing historical context surrounding federal crack and powder cocaine sentencing policies.

## Project Components

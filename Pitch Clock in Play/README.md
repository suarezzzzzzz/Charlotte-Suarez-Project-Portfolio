# Pitch Clock in Play: Exploring the Impact of the Introduction of the Pitch Clock on MLB Pitchers

* **Partners**: Kevin Lynch, Larry Nguyen, Alejandro Zamudio*

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Practical Machine Learning course during my junior year at The University of Texas at Austin. The project investigated the impact of MLB's 2023 pitch clock implementation on pitcher performance using data from the 2022 and 2023 seasons.

To strengthen our analysis, I reached out to the Texas Longhorn Baseball coaching staff and connected my team with the Director of Analytics to discuss player evaluation. We incorporated the metrics, analytical approaches, and decision-making processes shared with us into our own analysis to better understand how changes in pitcher performance could be evaluated in practice.

## Research Question

**How did the introduction of the MLB pitch clock affect pitcher performance?**

We used pitch-by-pitch data, single-game data, and yearly aggregates collected through PyBaseball to compare pitchers' 2023 performance with their own performance during the 2022 season. We applied multiple statistical and machine learning techniques to identify patterns in pitcher performance and classify whether players improved or declined following the introduction of the pitch clock.
